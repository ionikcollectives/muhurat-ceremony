// The Ceremony

WHAT IS A MUHURAT?

The auspicious first moment — a ritual commencement where a film's symbolic first reel is launched through ceremony, prayer, and collective intention.

1🪔
Oil Lamp Lighting

The defining ceremonial moment — the chief guest lights the traditional lamp, officially commencing production.

2🎬
Exclusive Screenings

Awareness trailer, character, director, and producer documentaries — all premiering before industry guests.

3🎙️
Speeches & Addresses

Director, cast, writer, and producer each take the stage — sharing their vision for this bold cinematic work.

4📋
Agreement Handover

A formal signing ceremony marking the production partnership and official contractual commencement.

5💃
Cultural Performance

Traditional dance enriches the atmosphere, honouring the roots of Sri Lankan artistic heritage.

6🥂
Brunch & Media

A curated brunch enabling press interaction, industry networking, and maximum media exposure.
